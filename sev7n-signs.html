THE IDEA / 01

AUTOMATION
NEEDS DISCIPLINE.

Markets move quickly. A system should not turn that speed into uncontrolled activity. The concept behind SEV7N SIGNS begins with clear strategy rules, repeatable decisions, and limits defined before any execution.

The intention is to explore technology that makes a strategy more consistent and observable—not to promise returns or remove risk.

  • Risk limits defined before use
  • Testing before any live consideration
  • Human review and a clear stop mechanism
